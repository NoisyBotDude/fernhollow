About Fernhollow

Fernhollow is an award-winning garden design and build company based in Knutsford, Cheshire. Since 2014 we have designed, built and looked after gardens across Cheshire and South Manchester - from compact courtyards to full landscape transformations, commercial grounds and new-build plot landscaping. Two build crews, a design studio and a maintenance team, all in-house.

Hannah Whitlock founded Fernhollow in 2014 after ten years as a garden designer in Manchester. It started as one van and a designer's drawing board in a Mobberley spare room. Today we are a team of 18 people: a design studio, two build crews, a maintenance and planting team and our own office, working from yards in Knutsford and Altrincham.

Our design principles

  • Design for how the family actually uses the garden, season by season
  • Fewer, better materials - repeated across the scheme
  • Planting for four-season structure and wildlife value
  • Build it to last: correct sub-bases, drainage first

Our design language

Contemporary country: clean porcelain or sawn sandstone, oak, corten accents, soft naturalistic planting. We love a limited palette used generously: one paving, one timber, one metal, repeated so the garden reads as a whole. Planting is layered and loose - grasses, perennials and multi-stem trees - softened against crisp hard landscaping.

How we talk to you

Plain English, no jargon, and always a clear next step. You'll get one point of contact, a written programme and a weekly update while we're on site.

Sustainability

  • Peat-free compost and soil improvers only
  • Permeable surfaces and soakaways wherever we can - we keep rainwater on site
  • Planting chosen for wildlife value; no artificial grass in our designs
  • Inert waste recycled through our aggregates partner
