Our process

  1. Free call - Tell us about your garden, your budget and your timing. We'll be straight with you about whether we're the right fit.
  2. Design consultation & survey - We visit, measure and survey levels, access, drainage and services.
  3. Design & proposal - You receive a design, a fixed-price proposal broken down by section, and a programme.
  4. Build - Your own site lead, a weekly update and a project page with photos. 25% deposit, then stage payments.
  5. Handover & aftercare - A walk-round, a snag list, a handover pack and a 12-month plant / 5-year workmanship guarantee.
