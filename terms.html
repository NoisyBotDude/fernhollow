Terms & conditions of engagement

1. Scope of work

  • 1.1 We will carry out the works described in the accepted quotation and drawings. Anything not listed is excluded.
  • 1.2 Changes requested after acceptance are variations, priced and agreed in writing before we carry them out.
  • 1.3 Quantities marked 'provisional' or 'TBA' are estimates and will be adjusted at actual cost plus our standard margin.

2. Price and payment

  • 2.1 Prices are valid for 60 days from the quotation date and include VAT at 20% unless stated.
  • 2.2 We invoice 25% on acceptance, 35% at build start, 30% at mid-build valuation and 10% on practical completion.
  • 2.3 Invoices are payable within 7 days (14 days for the final invoice). Late payment may incur statutory interest.

3. Site access and services

  • 3.1 The client will provide access, an outside tap and a 240V supply during working hours.
  • 3.2 We will locate and mark services before digging but cannot be liable for unrecorded services.
  • 3.3 Skips and grab lorries may need a permit where parked on the highway; we arrange this at cost.

4. Programme and weather

  • 4.1 Start and finish dates are estimates. We will keep you informed of any changes.
  • 4.2 We do not lay paving or turf in frost or persistent heavy rain; lost days extend the programme.

5. Plants and guarantees

  • 5.1 Plants are guaranteed for 12 months where watered as advised; this excludes drought, vandalism and pests.
  • 5.2 Hard landscaping is guaranteed for 5 years against defects in workmanship.

6. Cancellation

  • 6.1 You may cancel within 14 days of acceptance; deposits for bespoke materials already ordered are non-refundable.
