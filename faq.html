Frequently asked questions

Do you cover my area?

We work across Cheshire East, Trafford, South Manchester, Warrington - roughly 25 miles from Knutsford.

What's your minimum project size?

Our minimum design & build project is around £12,000. For smaller jobs we're happy to recommend local firms.

How much does a garden design cost?

Design fees are typically 8-10% of the build budget, credited against the build if we construct it. Design-only packages start at £1,850.

How do payments work?

25% deposit on acceptance, 35% at build start, 30% at a mid-build valuation and 10% on practical completion. Invoices are due within 7 days (14 for the final invoice).

How far ahead are you booked?

Typically 6-10 weeks for builds. Our busiest months are March to September, so winter is a great time to design.

Do you offer guarantees?

Plants are guaranteed for 12 months when watered as advised, and hard landscaping for 5 years against defects in workmanship.

Are you insured?

Yes: £10m public & products liability and £10m employers' liability.

Do I need planning permission?

Most garden work doesn't, but front driveways must be permeable or drain to a soakaway. Garden rooms over 2.5m high near a boundary may need permission - we'll advise.
